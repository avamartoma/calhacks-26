# Iteration1 watcher

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

The watcher follows a local Iteration1 office's live state. When a task blocks, becomes ready for review or finishes (and whether it was verified), when an approval is waiting, when a mission starts or when the office pauses, it messages its companion **iteration1-office** agent. That agent forwards the update to its paired owners in ASI:One.

The watcher runs on the office owner's computer beside the office agent. It takes no commands; talk to iteration1-office instead.
