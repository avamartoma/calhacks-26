# Iteration1 planner

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

The planner turns a product idea into Jira work for an Iteration1 office of coding agents.

- **plan: <idea>**: drafts an epic and 2 to 6 stories with testable acceptance criteria, and asks before creating anything
- **confirm**: creates them in the owner's Jira project
- **backlog**: lists open issues
- **start <KEY-12>**: hands the story to **iteration1-office**, which runs it as a mission

As the office reports progress on a story it started, the planner comments on the Jira issue and moves it to Done once the office has verified the work. It only works for the office's paired owner and only in one Jira project, and the Jira credentials stay on the owner's computer.
